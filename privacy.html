Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 18 October 2026

What the FBI UIU bot processes, what appears publicly, and how to request access, correction or deletion.

1. Operator and scope

This policy covers the FBI UIU roleplay bot and its companion website, operated through the project account s1nfullxst. Contact the bot operator through the FBI UIU Discord server; ask an administrator for a private contact route.

2. Information processed

  • Discord identifiers and permissions: user IDs, configured server/channel/role IDs, and role or permission information needed to authorize commands. Display names and role names may be used in replies and announcements.
  • Case records: titles, descriptions, status, resolutions, assigned user IDs, notes, evidence URLs, authors and timestamps.
  • Operations: titles, briefings, games, start times, status, creator IDs, sign-ups and staff-confirmed attendance.
  • Administrative history: command-related audit actions, actor IDs, promotion targets and role IDs, reasons, and relevant timestamps.
  • Community statistics: approximate server member counts, including bots, case totals and operation/attendance totals.

Information comes from your command submissions, Discord interactions, authorized staff entries and Discord's API. The bot does not implement general message-history collection, voice recording or evidence-file downloads. Evidence URLs themselves are stored. Do not submit credentials or sensitive real-world information in roleplay records.

3. Why the information is used

Records support case management, role changes, event planning, sign-ups, attendance, member summaries, permission checks, troubleshooting and restoring backups. The public website uses selected schedule fields and aggregate statistics. The bot does not implement advertising, data sales or AI model training with these records.

4. Who can see it

Case details, evidence links and attendance lists are restricted by the bot's staff permission checks. Members can view their own profile and assignments. Operation briefings are available through the bot to server members; promotion and operation announcements follow the configured channel's access permissions.

The public stats endpoint exposes approximate member counts, open/closed case totals and, with the dashboard update installed, upcoming operation IDs, titles, games and times, completed-operation totals and verified-attendance totals. It does not expose member IDs, attendance names, operation briefings or private case text. Published website archive reports are separate public content and may be copied or indexed by others.

People with bot-hosting or database access can access stored records. Administrators with Manage Server permission can export the full database using the backup command, including private case and attendance records. These exports must be kept private.

5. Service providers and storage

The bot stores records in a persistent SQLite database on Bot-Hosting. Discord processes interactions, replies and backup attachments. GitHub Pages hosts the public website and GitHub stores the website source and published content. These providers may process network information such as IP addresses and request logs under their own policies. Data may be processed outside your country, depending on their infrastructure.

The public API uses HTTPS. Application permissions restrict access within Discord, but the database and exported backup files are not separately encrypted by the bot code. Hosting-level encryption has not been independently verified. The bot's API access logger is disabled; hosting-provider logging is separate.

6. Retention and backups

The current software has no automatic record-expiry schedule. Closing a case, completing an operation, leaving the server or removing the bot does not erase the database or its audit history. Records persist until manually deleted or anonymized by the operator.

Records should be retained only while needed for the stated features. The operator must promptly handle valid deletion requests and remove data that is no longer needed or when the service ends, except where retention is legally required. Deletion handling must include controlled backup copies and prevent erased records being restored. Copies downloaded by administrators or posted on Discord require separate handling; deleting the database alone does not remove those copies.

7. Access, correction and deletion

Contact the bot operator privately through the support server and say you have a privacy request. Include your Discord user ID and the type of request: access, correction, export or deletion. Never send a password, bot token or identity document. The operator may verify control of the relevant Discord account before disclosing records.

Requests are handled manually; there is currently no self-service deletion command. Staff must check records, audit entries, free-text references, exports and backups, not just your profile. If a request cannot be fulfilled in full, the operator should explain why. Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, portability, and to complain to your local data-protection authority.

8. Website and third-party links

The site does not add advertising trackers or analytics cookies. Your browser contacts the hosting providers to load pages and refresh public statistics. Discord invites and evidence links lead to third-party services; their own privacy practices apply.

9. Updates

This page will be updated when the bot's data practices change. Material changes should also be announced in the community server. See the Terms of Service for rules on using the bot.
